# Third-party model attribution

BiteWise uses the pretrained FoodVision Big model by shalev396, hosted at
https://huggingface.co/shalev396/foodvision-big . The weights are downloaded
separately and are not included in this repository. The upstream model
is declared MIT licensed. Its license is reproduced below. Torchvision
provides the EfficientNet architecture and preprocessing.

The Food-101 training images and FoodSG-233 dataset are not redistributed.
The model is a general Food-101 baseline, not a BiteWise-trained Singapore
food classifier. The model author's reported metrics are not BiteWise evaluation results.
